About Normmed

Four decades of expertise, built into every implant.

Normmed Medical transferred almost 40 years of sector experience into production in 2011, building a modern, technology-driven factory in the İvedik Organized Industrial Zone of Ankara, Turkey.

We are a vertically integrated manufacturer of orthopedic implants and surgical instruments spanning spine, trauma, arthroscopy and arthroplasty. With more than 2,000 catalog items — and the flexibility for fully customized production — we deliver complete solutions from design and machining to sterilization and global logistics.

  • In-house R&D and CNC manufacturing
  • Full traceability and medical-grade materials
  • Customized production for surgeon-specific needs
  • Comprehensive instrument sets for every system

Featured systems

Signature products

Trimi™ Anterior Cervical Plate

High strength at a low 2 mm profile, with a flexible blocking system that secures directly to the screw — effective and simple to use.

Z-Rhythm™ Laminoplasty Plate

Designed to increase canal space with an easy-release screwdriver. Five plate sizes and two screw types (self-drilling and rescue).

Vertelift-C™ Corpectomy Cage

Complete contact with the vertebral surface, threaded surface for secure fixation, and efficient grafting space before distraction.

Innovation

A world first in implant safety.

Normmed Medical is the first company in the world to implement Antibacterial Silver-Ion Coated Implant Systems — engineering implants that help reduce the risk of surgical-site infection at the surface level.

It's part of a relentless focus on materials science, surface technology and surgeon-driven design that keeps us ahead of the field.
