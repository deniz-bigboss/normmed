Corporate · About Us

Four decades of know-how, built into every implant.

NORMMED Medikal transferred its almost 20 years of experience in the sector to production in 2011, setting up a modern, technology-based factory in the Ankara İvedik Organized Industrial Zone. It utilized its market experience and sector knowledge for new designs through a world-standard factory organization, and established its equipment park with future-oriented planning.

Aware of its responsibility for human health, NORMMED maintains a production approach that never compromises on quality and inspection — aiming to become a worldwide-known company through experienced staff, a strong capital structure, and continuously improving knowledge and machine technologies.

  • Manufactures all implants and auxiliary materials in-house
  • Designs and produces the surgical sets required to place them
  • Customer-oriented, strategic and systematic management
  • The only Silver-Coated Spinal Implant System maker in Turkey

Mission & Vision

All Product Range

Complete implant & instrument systems

Four product groups — with the surgical sets to place every one of them.

Spine

Spinal implant systems — cervical to sacral, including our Silver-Coated Spinal Implant System.

  • Pedicle screws
  • Trimi cervical plate
  • Z-Rhythm laminoplasty
  • Vertelift-C cage
  • Oxis Plus occipitocervical
  • PEEK / interbody cages
  • MIS systems

Trauma

A full range of anatomic locking plates and fixation for reliable fracture management.

  • LCP plates (Ø3.5 / Ø4.5)
  • Proximal humeral
  • Distal volar radius
  • Dynamic hip screw (DHS)
  • Distal femoral
  • Pelvic plates
  • Calcaneal
  • + 40 anatomic plates

Arthroscopy

Suture anchor systems for minimally invasive soft-tissue repair.

  • Titanium anchors
  • Headless titanium
  • PEEK anchors
  • Knotless PEEK
  • Knotless PEEK + Ti
  • All-suture anchors

Arthroplasty

Total joint replacement systems with complete instrumentation.

  • Cementless stem
  • Cemented stem
  • CoCr femoral head
  • Acetabular cup
  • Acetabular liner 0° / 20°
  • Acetabular screw
  • Centraliser
Signature systems

Featured spine products

Trimi™ Anterior Cervical Plate

High strength at a low 2 mm profile, with a flexible blocking system that secures directly to the screw — effective and simple to use.

Z-Rhythm™ Laminoplasty Plate

Designed to increase canal space with an easy-release screwdriver. Five plate sizes and two screw types (self-drilling and rescue).

Vertelift-C™ Corpectomy Cage

Complete contact with the vertebral surface, a threaded surface for secure fixation, and efficient grafting space before distraction.

Oxis Plus™ Occipitocervical System

Occipitocervical fixation engineered for stability across the craniocervical junction with a versatile set of connectors.
